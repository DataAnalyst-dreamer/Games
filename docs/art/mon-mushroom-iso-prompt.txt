Orthographic, NO perspective convergence, NO vanishing point. Light comes from the top-left. Crisp hard-edged pixels, NO anti-aliasing, NO blur, NO gradients, NO glow. Flat shading with exactly 3 tones per surface. Cute, sleepy, stationary proportions, warm and hand-made feel. No text, no watermark, no signature, no UI. Transparent background.

Ground contact at the bottom-center of a 32x32 logical pixel canvas, creature fills about 70% of canvas height, centered.

Sleepy half-closed eyes visible only on the front face, tucked under the cap rim. No arms.
