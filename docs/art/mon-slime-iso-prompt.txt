STYLE: 16-bit style pixel art for a cozy medieval fantasy island RPG. TRUE ISOMETRIC view: 2:1 dimetric projection on a 45-degree diamond grid. Orthographic, NO perspective convergence, NO vanishing point. Light comes from the top-left. Crisp hard-edged pixels, NO anti-aliasing, NO blur, NO gradients, NO glow. Flat shading with exactly 3 tones per surface. Cute, round, harmless proportions, warm and hand-made feel. No text, no watermark, no signature, no UI. Transparent background.

CREATURE RULES: This monster is radially near-symmetric (a round blob), so we only need a FRONT (S) pose and a RIGHT-side (E) pose — the other directions reuse these two via engine-side flips, no full 8-direction set needed. Ground contact at the bottom-center of a 32x32 logical pixel canvas, creature fills about 70% of canvas height, centered.

A round bouncy slime monster, "방울 슬라임" (dew-drop slime), the friendliest creature in the Hartland region. Translucent-looking teal jelly body: base #79b8ce, shadow #548789, highlight #71ddee, a lighter rim #abc2bc where light passes through the edge. Two simple round dark eyes and a small closed happy mouth on the front face only (no face on the back/side silhouette beyond a slight bulge). A slight flattened, wider bottom where it touches the ground (weight resting on the floor, not a perfect circle). No teeth, no claws, no menacing shapes — stays inside this project's "cute and harmless" silhouette rule.

Deliver two separate 1024x1024 images: (1) FRONT view facing the camera (S), eyes and mouth visible; (2) RIGHT side view facing east (E), body silhouette only, no face details needed on this side. Both at the same scale and same colors.
